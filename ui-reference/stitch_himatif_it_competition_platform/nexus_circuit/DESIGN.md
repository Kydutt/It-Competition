## Brand & Style
The design system orchestrates an electric, disciplined technological arena tailored for student innovators, engineers, and competitive coders across the Ciayumajakuning region. It bridges the high-velocity adrenaline of hackathon culture with the rigorous operational precision of enterprise developer tools and conference portals. 

The aesthetic merges **Modern SaaS Precision** with **Futuristic Circuit Geometry**. Surfaces remain deep, clean, and intentionally structured—avoiding chaotic maximalism while injecting hyper-focused moments of electrical kinetic energy through vivid orange focal points, delicate luminous borders, and micro-grid patterns.

Key visual principles:
- **Disciplined Velocity:** Complex workflows—like multi-stage team submissions, repository links, judge scoring matrixes, and live telemetry—feel swift and frictionless.
- **Architectural Depth:** Deep midnight layered backgrounds paired with hairline borders (0.5px to 1px) evoke technical schematics, physical silicon wafers, and IDE interfaces.
- **Electric Intentionality:** Vibrant orange (#F97316) is reserved exclusively for pivotal actions, active competitive states, countdown milestones, and earned achievement badges, avoiding sensory fatigue.

## Colors
The system is built on an authoritative dark-first canvas optimized for intensive multi-hour sessions, high-density data tables, and developer dashboard interfaces.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#F97316` / `#EA580C` / `#FFEDD5`):** Electric Circuit Orange. Drives call-to-actions, registration deadlines, submission buttons, current step wizards, and active progress trackers. Hover states transition to `#EA580C` with subtle radial glows (`rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.15)`).
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / `#172554` / `#1E3A8A`):** Deep Midnight & Navy. Powers elevated surface containers, active navigation rail panels, table headers, and structural card envelopes.
- **Tertiary (`#3B82F6` / `#2563EB`):** Cyber Cyan & Indigo. Reserved for metadata tags, technical category pills (e.g., IoT, Competitive Programming, UI/UX), system telemetry, and secondary contextual links.
- **Neutral Palette (`#020617`, `#09090B`, `#0F172A`, `#334155`, `#94A3B8`, `#F8FAFC`):**
  - **Background Canvas (`#020617`):** Primary viewport backdrop.
  - **Surface Default (`#09090B` / `#0F172A`):** Card bodies, modals, and panel drawers.
  - **Surface High (`#1E293B`):** Dropdowns, popovers, and interactive rows on hover.
  - **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** High-contrast headlines and critical values.
  - **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Explanatory labels, timestamps, and table metadata.
  - **Border Subdued (`rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)` or `#1E293B`):** Fine circuit partitions.

### Functional Status Indicators
- **Success (`#22C55E` / `#16A34A`):** Passed test cases, submitted milestones, verified registration status.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Pending reviews, closing-soon alerts, file size approaching limits.
- **Error (`#EF4444` / `#DC2626`):** Disqualification notices, validation errors, missed submission deadlines.
- **Info (`#3B82F6`):** Announcements, judge guidelines, protocol documentation.

## Typography
The system deploys **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and brand-anchored elements to convey geometric, crisp forward-momentum. **Inter** handles high-utility body content, complex tabular data, and UI controls for maximal legibility across diverse device viewports.

### Numerical & Tabular Data
For hackathon countdown clocks, scoring leaderboards, and file transfer metrics, activate OpenType tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; font-feature-settings: "tnum"`). This prevents jittering during dynamic tick operations and aligns multi-tier scorecards cleanly down grid columns.

### Text Treatments
- **All-Caps Labels (`label-sm`):** Transform to uppercase with an expanded tracking of `+0.04em` for technical badges, column headers, and track categories.
- **Section Badges:** Paired with an inline dot indicator, rendered in `label-sm` semibold styling.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements an asymmetric 12-column fluid grid system paired with fixed dashboard frame layouts:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, `gutter` at `1.5rem` (24px), page margin at `2rem` (32px), max content width capped at `1440px`. Dedicated 260px collapsible sidebar for administrative and participant consoles.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `gutter` at `1.25rem` (20px), outer margin at `1.5rem` (24px). Sidebar minimizes into a persistent utility icon dock (64px width).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout, `gutter-mobile` at `1rem` (16px), `margin-mobile` at `1rem` (16px). Navigation collapses to an accessible bottom sticky tab bar or sliding sheet.

### Rhythmic Discipline
Horizontal and vertical alignment leverages an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Card contents utilize uniform `1.5rem` (24px) internal padding, reducing to `1rem` (16px) on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy, muddy drop-shadows. Instead, depth is constructed via **Tonal Stacking**, **Glassmorphism**, and **Luminescent Edges**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Pure deep midnight `#020617`. May feature a faint background SVG grid pattern (`stroke: rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.04); 32px grid pitch`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Dark slate surface `#0B132B` or `#0F172A` with a 1px continuous hairline outline `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Dropdowns, Hovered Cards):** Surface elevated to `#1E293B`, reinforced by a sharp hairline border `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.35)` when focused or active, paired with a diffused ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.8), 0 0 20px -5px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Step Overlays & Drawers):** Heavy backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), background fill `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`, and structural rim illumination `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.

## Shapes
The system relies on a unified **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard:
- Standard UI interactive components (buttons, text fields, chips) feature `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- Cards, modal containers, and dashboard analytical widgets employ `rounded-xl` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Badges, status pills, and toggle indicators apply complete pill curvature (`9999px`) to immediately contrast against structural block cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid vibrant Electric Orange (`#F97316`) background, text `#020617` (bold, optimal contrast). On hover: `#EA580C` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.4)`). Active state scales slightly to `0.98`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill with a 1px border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)` and text `#F8FAFC`. On hover: surface changes to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` and border transitions to `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.5)`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, transparent background, text `#94A3B8`. Hover yields `#F8FAFC` with subtle background tint `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact layout (`py-1 px-2.5`, `rounded-full`, `label-sm`).
  - *Active / Live:* Background `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.15)`, text `#FB923C`, border `1px solid rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.3)`. Accompanied by a pulsing 6px circle dot.
  - *Verified / Qualified:* Background `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.15)`, text `#4ADE80`, border `1px solid rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.3)`.
  - *Category Tags (IoT, Web, Mobile):* Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)`, text `#CBD5E1`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- Default state features `#0B132B` solid or semi-translucent (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`) background with a 1px border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.1)`.
- Hoverable submission cards smoothly transition border color to `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.4)` and translate `translateY(-2px)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#0F172A`, 1px border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#475569`. Internal padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts sharply to `#F97316` accompanied by an electric halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.2)`). Outline is suppressed.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom controls styled with `0.25rem` (4px) roundedness for checkboxes and complete circular radiuses for radios. Unchecked: 1.5px border `#334155` on `#0F172A`. Checked: `#F97316` solid fill with crisp `#020617` checkmark icon or center pip.

### Interactive Trackers & Step Wizards
- **Horizontal Stepper:** Numbered circular nodes (32px) connected by 2px hairline rails. Finished steps render with `#22C55E` fill and checkmarks; current stage pulses with `#F97316` and text in `title-md`; upcoming steps remain muted `#334155` with `#64748B` labels.

### Data Tables (Leaderboard & Submissions)
- Clean, unbordered exterior shell with light horizontal dividers `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`. Table head uses uppercase `label-sm` in `#64748B` on `#09090B`. Row hover activates `#1E293B` background transition. Score cells utilize `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
