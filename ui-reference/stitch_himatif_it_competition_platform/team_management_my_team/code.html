HIMATIF IT Competition 2026 Manual

1. Institutional Representation

Teams may combine active students from different institutions within the Ciayumajakuning region (Kabupaten Cirebon, Kota Cirebon, Indramayu, Majalengka, Kuningan). Each member must possess a valid Kartu Tanda Mahasiswa (KTM) active for the 2025/2026 academic calendar.

2. Quota & Role Allotment

A squad is declared legally registered upon reaching at least two (2) verified members. The upper cap is rigidly bound to three (3) members. Any individual can only represent one team across the entire competition period.

3. Stage 1 Submission Deliverables

Preliminary round requires: Design Executive Brief (PDF, max 10 pages), interactive Figma link with public viewing permissions, and recorded 3-minute video walkthrough pitched by team members.

4. Disqualification Criteria

Submitting pre-existing commercial projects, utilizing unaccredited external contributors, or failure to produce valid institutional verification prior to the final judging round will yield immediate disqualification with zero fee refunds.
